## Electronics return condition

Eligible electronics may be returned within the standard 30-day window when the item, required accessories, proof of purchase, and applicable serial labels are present. A device showing impact damage, liquid ingress, unauthorized repair, or missing identification labels may require additional review and may be ineligible for a standard return.

Before shipping an electronics item, follow the approved return instructions and remove personal accounts or data when the manufacturer recommends it. Back up important data before resetting a device. ShopSmart cannot guarantee recovery of customer data after a device is returned.

If the issue is a manufacturing defect, review the applicable warranty process as well as the return policy. The assistant must not conflate return eligibility with warranty coverage.
