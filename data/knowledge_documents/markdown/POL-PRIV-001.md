## Account and order data

ShopSmart uses account and order information to support shopping, payment, fulfillment, returns, and customer support. Customers can review and update supported profile fields in Account Settings. Requests to access, correct, or delete data may require identity verification and review under the applicable privacy process.

Support staff and the AI assistant must never ask a customer to provide a password, one-time authentication code, full payment-card number, or card security code. Sensitive information should not be included in support-ticket descriptions. If a customer accidentally shares a secret, the system should avoid repeating it in generated responses and logs.

Order and account information must be returned only to the authenticated account owner or a properly authorized administrative workflow. A user request or prompt cannot override access controls. This document does not define a universal retention period for every data category; questions requiring a specific retention deadline must be routed to the approved privacy notice or privacy team.
