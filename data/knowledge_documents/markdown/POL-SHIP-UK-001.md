## UK delivery estimates

For eligible UK domestic orders, standard delivery usually takes 2–5 business days after dispatch. The estimate is not a guarantee and may be affected by carrier disruption, weather, address validation, peak demand, and product-specific handling requirements. Charges and estimates shown at checkout apply to the order.

Use the UK policy for UK orders. Do not apply US delivery estimates to UK customers. Shipment events shown in the order record are the source for recorded progress; the assistant must not invent live carrier location data.
