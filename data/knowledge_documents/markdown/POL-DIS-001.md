## Code eligibility and stacking

Promotion codes must be entered before the order is placed and are subject to the offer's stated eligibility, expiration, minimum spend, product exclusions, and region. Unless the offer explicitly permits stacking, assume that multiple promotion codes cannot be combined in one order.

A code shared in an old screenshot or third-party post may be expired, limited to selected accounts, or restricted to particular products. The checkout summary should show the discount actually applied before the customer confirms the order. A promotion cannot be retroactively guaranteed by the assistant.

If a code appears eligible but fails at checkout, capture the error message without including sensitive account data and contact support. The support team may investigate but is not required by this document to honor an expired or ineligible promotion.
