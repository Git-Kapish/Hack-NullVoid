## Packaging and recycling

Where available, product pages identify recyclable packaging, take-back programs, or battery-disposal guidance. Recycling rules vary by municipality and material type. Customers should consult local guidance for batteries, electronics, packaging foam, and other materials rather than assuming all components belong in ordinary household recycling.

Rechargeable batteries and electronic devices should not be placed in general recycling unless local rules explicitly permit it. Keep damaged or swollen batteries away from heat and follow local hazardous-waste instructions. Product packaging claims apply only to the materials and locations described on the relevant product page.

This guide is general demo guidance, not a jurisdiction-specific legal statement. If a customer asks for an exact local collection point or legal requirement, retrieve an approved region-specific source or abstain.
