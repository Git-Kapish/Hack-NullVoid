## Prime shipping benefits

Eligible Prime members receive standard shipping at no additional shipping charge on qualifying items when the product page and checkout identify the item as Prime-eligible. Eligibility is determined per item and destination, not solely by the customer's membership tier.

Oversized products, restricted goods, special-handling items, selected marketplace listings, and some remote destinations may carry separate charges or delivery terms. The checkout summary is the source of truth for the benefit applied to a particular order. Prime membership does not guarantee a specific delivery date unless a separate, explicit delivery promise appears in the order confirmation.

Membership status must be checked from the authenticated account record. A customer saying that they are Prime is not sufficient evidence for a privileged benefit. Benefits can change for future orders when membership status or terms change; the terms applicable to an existing order should be evaluated using the relevant order and policy version.
