## Laptop warranty coverage

Eligible laptops sold as new include a limited manufacturer warranty for 12 months from purchase unless the exact product listing states another term. Coverage generally addresses manufacturing defects during normal use. Accidental damage, liquid ingress, unauthorized repair, and normal wear are excluded unless the model-specific terms explicitly say otherwise.

Keep the order receipt and product serial number for an authorized claim. The assistant should not promise repair or replacement without an eligibility decision. The general smartphone warranty policy must not be treated as evidence of a laptop-specific warranty when the product-specific policy is available.
