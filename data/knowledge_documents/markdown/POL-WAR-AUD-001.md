## Audio warranty coverage

Eligible audio products sold as new include a limited manufacturer warranty for 12 months from purchase unless the product listing specifies a different duration. Coverage generally addresses manufacturing defects under normal use. Damage caused by misuse, liquid exposure beyond the product rating, unauthorized modification, or normal wear may be excluded.

The exact product and purchase record must be checked before determining eligibility. This policy does not guarantee replacement or a particular repair turnaround. Use the manufacturer-specific terms if the product listing identifies a different warranty.
