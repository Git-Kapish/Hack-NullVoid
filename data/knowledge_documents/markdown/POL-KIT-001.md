## Heat compatibility and cleaning

Confirm whether a kitchenware item is suitable for a dishwasher, microwave, oven, stovetop, or induction surface using the specific product label or manual. Do not infer heat compatibility from appearance, material color, or a generic category description.

Allow hot cookware to cool safely and use suitable protection when handling it. Follow cleaning instructions to avoid damage to coatings, seals, or electrical components. Discontinue use if the item cracks, warps, or develops damage that could affect safe use.

If the product's specific heat rating is absent from the knowledge base, the assistant should not guess. It should request the exact model or recommend checking the product label.
