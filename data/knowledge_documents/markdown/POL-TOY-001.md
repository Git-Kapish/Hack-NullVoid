## Age labels and small parts

Follow the age grading, warning labels, and assembly instructions supplied with each toy. Keep small parts away from children when the packaging identifies a choking hazard. Inspect toys periodically for loose components, sharp edges, broken seams, or damaged batteries.

Stop using a toy if a component breaks or a safety feature no longer works. Battery compartments should remain secured as described in the manual. Adult assembly or supervision may be required for certain products; check the exact product packaging instead of inferring suitability from appearance.

This guide is not a substitute for the warnings supplied with an individual product. If the age range or safety rating is missing from the available records, the assistant should ask for the exact product or abstain.
