## Coverage and exclusions

Eligible smartphones sold as new by ShopSmart include a limited manufacturer warranty for 12 months from the purchase date unless the product listing or included manufacturer terms state a different duration. The warranty generally covers manufacturing defects arising during normal use and is administered according to the applicable manufacturer's terms.

Coverage generally excludes accidental drops, cracked screens caused by impact, liquid ingress, unauthorized repairs or modifications, normal cosmetic wear, consumables, and damage caused by incompatible accessories. These exclusions do not replace any non-waivable rights that may apply under local law.

To start a claim, retain the order receipt and device serial or IMEI information and use the authorized warranty-support route. Do not publish serial numbers or other device identifiers in a public support forum. A warranty claim is subject to eligibility review; the policy does not guarantee replacement, repair, or a particular turnaround time.
