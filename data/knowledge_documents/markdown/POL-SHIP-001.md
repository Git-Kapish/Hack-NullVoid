## Delivery estimates and shipping charges

Shipping charges and estimated delivery dates are displayed at checkout before the customer places an order. Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 business days after dispatch for eligible domestic orders. This estimate is not a guarantee and excludes the time an order spends in payment review or warehouse processing.

Delivery timing may change because of weather, carrier disruption, address validation, peak demand, restricted destinations, or other events outside ShopSmart's control. The shipment timeline in Order History is the primary place to view recorded events. An estimate is calculated from dispatch, not from the moment an item is added to the cart.

Some oversized, hazardous, restricted, third-party, or special-handling products may have different charges or delivery methods. The final shipping amount is the amount shown on the order confirmation. If an address is incorrect, contact support promptly; a carrier may not be able to redirect a parcel once it is in transit.
