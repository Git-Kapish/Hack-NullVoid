## Safe use and load limits

Follow assembly steps, dimensions, and load limits supplied for the specific office product. Keep small components away from children and store cutting, stapling, or binding tools safely. Do not exceed stated weight limits for stands, shelves, chairs, or mounts.

Check supported paper sizes, refill types, connector requirements, and dimensions on the product detail page before purchase. Similar-looking accessories may not be compatible. If the manual does not specify a limit or supported component, do not infer it from a different product in the same category.
