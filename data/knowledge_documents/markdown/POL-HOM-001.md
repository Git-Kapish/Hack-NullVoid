## Electrical and cleaning safety

Place home appliances on a stable surface and follow the electrical, clearance, and ventilation requirements in the model-specific manual. Do not bypass safety interlocks. Unplug the appliance before cleaning when the manual instructs you to do so, and keep liquids away from components that are not designed for wet use.

If a product leaks, sparks, emits smoke, or produces an unusual burning odor, stop using it. Disconnect power only if doing so is safe and contact qualified service. Do not open sealed electrical components or attempt repairs without appropriate authorization.

Dishwasher, microwave, oven, and induction compatibility must be confirmed from the product label or model manual. Appearance alone does not establish heat resistance or electrical compatibility.
