## Basic tier limitations

Basic members can use standard catalog, account, cart, and order features available to authenticated customers. Shipping charges, delivery estimates, and promotion eligibility are shown at checkout and depend on the item, destination, and active offer.

Benefits explicitly restricted to Prime or Premium membership do not apply to Basic members unless the offer expressly includes Basic. The assistant must verify the membership tier from the authenticated account record rather than accepting a tier supplied only in chat.

A membership tier does not override item-level restrictions, stock availability, regional policy, or order state. The checkout summary determines which benefits apply to the particular purchase.
