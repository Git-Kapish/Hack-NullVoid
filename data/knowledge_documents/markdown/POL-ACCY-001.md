## Checking accessory compatibility

Customers should check the product's published compatibility list before buying chargers, cables, docks, batteries, mounts, or other accessories. Connector shape alone does not prove compatibility. Power rating, supported protocol, device generation, dimensions, firmware, and manufacturer restrictions may also matter.

Use the specifications for the exact product model and variant. If compatibility is not documented, ask support or consult the manufacturer's manual rather than assuming that the accessory will work. Avoid using a damaged charger, swollen battery, or accessory that becomes unusually hot.
