## Display connection and cleaning

Use a supported video cable and a resolution/refresh-rate combination listed for the monitor and source device. The maximum refresh rate may depend on the port, cable, graphics hardware, resolution, and configuration. Check the model specification rather than assuming every connector supports every advertised mode.

Keep ventilation openings clear and clean the panel with an approved soft cloth. Do not spray cleaning liquid directly onto the screen. Image retention behavior and prevention steps depend on panel type and usage pattern; consult the specific manual for supported care recommendations.

The assistant should not guarantee a particular refresh rate without checking the exact model and connection requirements.
