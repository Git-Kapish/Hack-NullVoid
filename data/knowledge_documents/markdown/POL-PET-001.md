## Selecting and inspecting pet products

Choose pet products according to the animal's size, age, chewing behavior, and intended use. Inspect toys, collars, leads, and feeding accessories regularly for wear and remove damaged items. Follow product-label instructions for cleaning, assembly, and use.

Do not assume that a product is suitable for every species or size. If an animal ingests a component or shows an adverse reaction, contact a veterinarian. Product listings and manuals should be used to confirm material, size, and intended-use information.

The assistant must not provide a veterinary diagnosis or claim a product is medically safe without specific approved evidence.
