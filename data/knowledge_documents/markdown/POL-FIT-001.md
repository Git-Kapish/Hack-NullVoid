## Wearable fit and metric limitations

Wear the fitness tracker according to the fit guidance and clean it after heavy sweating. Water resistance is model-specific and can decrease with wear, impact, or damage; do not assume that every tracker is suitable for swimming. Follow the exact product manual for charging and cleaning.

Heart rate, sleep, activity, and calorie estimates can be affected by fit, movement, sensor conditions, and the algorithm used. These values are estimates, not medical measurements or diagnoses. Seek qualified medical advice for health concerns rather than relying on a consumer wearable.

If the tracker overheats, the battery swells, or the enclosure is damaged, stop using it and contact authorized support.
