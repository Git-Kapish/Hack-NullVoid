## Reporting damage or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, defective on arrival, or different from the item ordered, report the issue through Order History or the Help Center as soon as practical. Include the order number and a clear description. Support may request photographs of the item, packaging, shipping label, or visible damage to assess the claim.

Keep the packaging and accessories until support provides instructions. Do not attempt to repair a damaged electrical product or continue using a product that appears unsafe. If it is safe to do so, disconnect power from a product that sparks, smokes, or becomes unusually hot.

Support reviews the order details and available evidence before authorizing a return, replacement, or other remedy. This document does not promise automatic replacement or a specific compensation amount. The assistant may help create a ticket but cannot approve a remedy unless a separately authorized backend workflow explicitly permits it.
