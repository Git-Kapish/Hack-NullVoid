## Creating and tracking a support ticket

Customers can create a support ticket through the Help Center. Include a concise subject, a description of the issue, the relevant order number if applicable, and the outcome being requested. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, full payment-card details, or unnecessary personal information.

A ticket acknowledgement confirms that the request was received; it does not mean that a refund, replacement, cancellation, or warranty claim has been approved. Response times depend on queue volume and issue complexity. Customers can use the ticket view to review status and provide additional information when requested.

Tickets are private to the authenticated customer and authorized support roles. The backend must enforce ownership checks. The AI assistant may create a ticket only through a validated tool call and should confirm success only after the backend returns a successful result.
