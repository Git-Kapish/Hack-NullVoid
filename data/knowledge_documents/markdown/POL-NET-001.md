## Secure router configuration

Place the router in a ventilated location away from major obstructions and sources of interference. During setup, change default administrator credentials and use the strongest security mode supported by the router and connected devices. Install firmware updates through the manufacturer's official process.

Wireless performance depends on the internet service plan, radio interference, building materials, device capability, router placement, and network load. A router cannot increase the speed provided by the internet service plan. If connectivity fails, check power, service status, cable connections, and the documented indicator lights before resetting the device.

Do not share administrator passwords or recovery codes in a support ticket. A factory reset may erase custom network settings, so use it only after reviewing the model's instructions.
